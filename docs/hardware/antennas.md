## Selection notes

- Small portable devices usually benefit from compact, rugged antennas rather than maximum gain.
- Fixed sites need careful EIRP, mounting and lightning/weather considerations.
- Higher gain narrows the vertical radiation pattern and can be worse for nearby or valley coverage.
- Community field-testing should drive any "recommended" label.
