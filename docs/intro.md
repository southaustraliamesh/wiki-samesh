![SA:MUG banner](/img/samug-logo.png)

SA Mesh is the South Australian MeshCore community knowledge base: practical local notes, repeatable setup guidance and links to the live SA dashboard and map.

This Docusaurus wiki is being migrated from the SA:MUG Wiki.js content. MeshCore pages have been moved first because MeshCore is the current SA Mesh priority. Public pages should remain general-user friendly and conservative until settings are field-tested.
:::
